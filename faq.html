Frequently asked questions

Do I need an app or a subscription?

No. PetFind opens as a normal web page in any browser. There's nothing to install for you or for the person who finds your pet, and the QR code works even if a GPS collar's battery is dead.

Is my home address shown to strangers?

Never. Only your phone number and the nearest vet clinic are shown on the page. Your home address stays private for your family's safety.

How does the "nearest vet" work?

With the finder's permission, the page reads their phone's location and looks up the closest veterinary clinic using OpenStreetMap. That means the vet shown is near wherever your pet actually is — not a fixed address you set once. If the finder declines location, the page shows a backup vet (if you added one) or a "search vets near me" link.

Can I choose what a finder should do?

Yes. When you create your tag you pick the steps a finder sees — from friendly suggestions like "keep me calm" or "offer me water," or in your own words. You can reorder, edit, or remove any of them.

What information do I need to provide?

Just your pet's name and your phone number to start. Everything else — breed, age, photo caption, backup vet — is optional.

Does this replace the mandatory microchip?

No. In France, identifying dogs, cats and ferrets with an I-CAD-registered microchip is legally required. PetFind is complementary: it gives finders an instant, friendly way to reach you so you reunite faster.

Which languages are supported?

The pet page is available in English and French, and a finder can switch between them with one tap. More languages are planned.

Is PetFind free?

Setting up your pet's page and generating a QR code is free. Physical tags, premium features and the wider connected-care app are on the roadmap.

What happens if I change my phone number?

You update your details and generate a fresh page. Editable, always-up-to-date pages tied to a permanent short link are coming as we add accounts — see the roadmap on our home page.
