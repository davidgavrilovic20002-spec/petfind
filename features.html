Everything PetFind does, explained.

It starts with a paw tag that always works — no battery, no app. With a subscription it grows into five more functions that look after your pet’s whole life.

Included with your tag (one-time purchase) Premium (monthly subscription)

Always-on QR identity tag Included

The paw tag carries a unique QR code that needs no battery, no app and no account for the finder. Anyone who finds your pet scans it and instantly reaches you — your home address is never shown.

A finder scansAny phone camera opens your pet’s page — worldwide, in English or French.
One tap to reach youThey call or text you directly, and read the steps you wrote for them.
Nearest vet, liveThe page shows the closest clinic to wherever your pet turns up.

Live location & safe zones Premium

Pair a PetFind or partner tracker and see your pet on a live map. Draw safe zones — home, the garden, the park — and get an instant alert the moment your pet leaves one.

See them on a mapReal-time position and location history, right in your account.
Draw safe zonesSet the areas your pet is normally allowed to roam.
Get exit alertsA notification fires the second they cross a safe-zone edge.
Needs a compatible tracker — rolling out with our hardware partners.

Health vitals & trends Premium

Track weight, activity, heart and breathing rate over time — like a fitness band for your pet. PetFind spots unusual trends early and flags them so nothing slips by.

Log or auto-collectEnter measurements yourself, or let a connected device fill them in.
See the trendClear charts show what’s normal for your pet and what’s changing.
Early anomaly flagsGet a heads-up when a metric drifts out of the usual range.

Medical records & medication Premium

A digital vaccine passport and full history in one place. When your vet adds a prescription, PetFind turns it into a dosing schedule with reminders — and tracks which doses were given.

Vaccine passportVaccinations, diagnoses and documents kept together and shareable.
Medication scheduleReminders for every dose, built automatically from the prescription.
Adherence trackingTick off doses so you and your vet can see the treatment is on track.

Vet appointments & escalation Premium

Book appointments with partner clinics from the app. In an emergency, PetFind proposes the nearest available vet — but nothing is ever dispatched without your explicit confirmation.

Book in a tapRoutine, follow-up or home-visit requests to clinics near you.
Emergency helpThe nearest open clinic is surfaced fast when it matters most.
You stay in controlEscalation is always owner-confirmed — never automatic.

Smart bowls & feeders Premium

Connect a compatible smart feeder or water fountain and manage feeding from PetFind: schedule meals, feed remotely, and see how much your pet actually eats and drinks each day.

Schedule mealsSet portions and times; the feeder handles the rest.
Feed remotelyTrigger a meal or top up water from your phone.
Track intakeFeeding and drinking logs flow into the health picture.
Works with partner feeders — connected through their apps.
