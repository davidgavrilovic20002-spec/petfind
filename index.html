Why PetFind

Everything a finder needs, on one page.

Always-on QR

The paw tag carries a QR code that draws no power. It works forever as an ID fallback — even when a battery is flat.

One-tap contact

The finder calls or texts you directly from the page. No sign-up, no waiting, no middleman.

Nearest vet, live

The page finds the closest clinic to wherever your pet is found — not a fixed address — so help is always close.

Private by design

Your home address is never shown. Only your phone number and the nearest vet appear on the page.

You write the steps

Choose exactly what a finder should do — from friendly suggestions or your own words, in English or French.

Any phone, any language

Opens in any browser worldwide. Finders can switch between English and French with one tap.

How it works

Three steps to a safer pet.

1

Create your pet's page

Add your pet's details and your phone number, and pick what a finder should do. Takes about a minute.

2

Get your unique QR code

We generate a one-of-a-kind QR you can print at home or put on a tag. Every pet gets its own.

3

A finder scans, you reunite

Anyone who finds your pet scans the code, calls you, and sees the nearest vet — no app required.
